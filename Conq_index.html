How to Play Conq

Build an empire across the world map. Human players choose unique countries; every unchosen country becomes an AI power.

① Create or join
Use Solo for a local match, or Create Room / Join Room for multiplayer.
② Choose a country
Search the list or click the map. Lock your pick before the host launches.
③ Manage your economy
Use your treasury to recruit troops and fortify borders.
④ Conquer neighbors
Click your territory, then an adjacent enemy territory to attack.
⑤ End your turn
Once everyone finishes, income is generated and AI powers take their turns.
⑥ Explore the map
Switch layers to compare population, GDP, fertility and resources.
